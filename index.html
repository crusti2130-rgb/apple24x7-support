Troubleshooting & Maintenance Guide

Resolve common issues regarding button feedback, magnetic strength, and long-term material upkeep easily.

🛠️ Stiff Button Feedback Fix

If buttons feel slightly rigid initially, gently flex the outer silicone edges outward 2-3 times before installation. The flexible elastomer compound softens within hours of use for crisp, clicky feedback.

🧲 Weak Magnetic Attachment Check

Ensure your wireless charging pad or magnetic wallet matches standard MagSafe polarity. Third-party unauthorized charging rings can occasionally disrupt alignment strength.

💧 Cleaning Liquid Silicone Covers

Wipe dust and lint off using a microfiber cloth dampened with a few drops of water or isopropyl alcohol. Avoid harsh industrial chemical solvents.

🪵 Preserving Leather Patina

European leather naturally darkens and gains character over months of use. Apply occasional leather conditioning balm to prevent drying or cracking in extreme climates.
